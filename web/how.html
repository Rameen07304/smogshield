SmogShield

How SmogShield works

SmogShield: four cooperating agents that turn raw air quality data into real-time smog response.

The pipeline

  1. 1

    Monitor

    Fetches and cleans air quality readings.

    Receives
    A city, plus live data from Open-Meteo (or a demo scenario).
    Produces
    The last 6 hourly readings: PM2.5, PM10, the time, and whether they are live or simulated.

  2. 2

    Analysis

    Computes the AQI, the trend and sudden spikes.

    Receives
    The recent readings.
    Produces
    An AQI number, a category (Good to Hazardous), the trend per hour, a spike flag and a one-line reason.

  3. 3

    Advisory

    Turns the assessment into guidance for each audience, in English and اردو.

    Receives
    The assessment: category, trend and spike flag.
    Produces
    A headline and advice for the public, children and schools, the elderly, and people with heart or lung conditions.

  4. 4

    Alert

    Decides when and how to notify people, without spamming them.

    Receives
    The assessment, the advice and the location.
    Produces
    An info, warning or critical alert in the dashboard feed (and Telegram, if set up), or nothing when nothing changed.
